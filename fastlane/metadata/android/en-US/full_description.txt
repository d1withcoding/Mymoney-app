Ghana-focused personal finance tracking for MTN MoMo and Telecel Cash.

PennyWise reads supported mobile money SMS messages locally and organizes transactions in a searchable timeline. You can also add transactions manually.

★ Ghana mobile money
• Parse MTN MoMo and Telecel Cash transaction messages
• Record amounts in Ghana cedi (GHS)
• Review and categorize transactions in one place

★ Budgets and insights
• Set budgets and track spending by category
• View spending trends, category breakdowns and analytics
• Use local rules to categorize transactions

★ Private by design
• No account or sign-up required
• No ads or financial-data tracking
• Transaction data stays on your device
• Open source under AGPL-3.0
